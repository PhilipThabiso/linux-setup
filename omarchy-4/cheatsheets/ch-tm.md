# tmux Cheatsheet

### Sessions
**New Session**
`tmux new -s <name>`

**Attach Session**
`tmux a -t <name>`

**List Sessions**
`tmux ls`

**Detach**
`Ctrl+b` `d`

**Rename Session**
`Ctrl+b` `$`

---

### Windows
**Create Window**
`Ctrl+b` `c`

**Next Window**
`Ctrl+b` `n`

**Previous Window**
`Ctrl+b` `p`

**Select by Index**
`Ctrl+b` `<0-9>`

**Rename Window**
`Ctrl+b` `,`

**Close Window**
`Ctrl+b` `&`

---

### Panes
**Vertical Split**
`Ctrl+b` `%`

**Horizontal Split**
`Ctrl+b` `"`

**Navigate Panes**
`Ctrl+b` `<Arrow Keys>`

**Toggle Zoom**
`Ctrl+b` `z`

**Close Pane**
`Ctrl+b` `x`
